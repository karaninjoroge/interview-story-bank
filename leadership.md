## Notes on Leadership in PM Interviews

Leadership in PM interviews almost never means "I managed a team." For early-career PMs, it means:
- I identified a problem no one else was addressing and did something about it
- I got people moving in a direction without having authority over them
- I set a standard for how work should be done
- I took ownership when no one else would

Your biomedical engineering projects, coursework, and self-directed PM learning all contain leadership stories. The question is whether you're framing them correctly.
